About Real Life Safaris

A Kigali team that shows you the Rwanda it calls home.

We create authentic, tailor-made journeys across East Africa, working with local experts so every trip is safe, well organised and kind to the places you visit.

That means birding in the eastern wetlands where the Papyrus Gonolek lives, a morning with a beekeeping cooperative in the hills above the city, and safaris that cover both the south and the north of Akagera. You get the depth of a local guide with the organisation of a full-service operator.

  • IntegrityClear prices and honest advice.
  • SustainabilityEco-friendly stays and local guides.
  • Cultural respectResponsible time with communities.

What we do

Everything between landing in Kigali and flying home.

Tailor-made itineraries

Private or small-group trips planned around your dates, interests and pace, across Rwanda, Uganda, Tanzania and Kenya.

Day tours & weekend hikes

Guided group hikes, birding and nature walks around Kigali every Saturday and Sunday, plus full days from Musanze.

Safari game drives

Day and night game drives and boat trips in Akagera, in pop-up roof Toyota Land Cruisers built for wildlife viewing.

Birding expeditions

Specialist birding days in Akagera, Nyungwe and Kigali's wetlands, from the Shoebill to Albertine Rift endemics.

Culture & community

Beekeeping with a local cooperative, village walks, traditional cooking, coffee and tea visits, and lake life with fishermen.

Permits, lodging & logistics

Park permits, lodges, transport and airport pickups arranged for you, so the only thing to plan is what to pack.
